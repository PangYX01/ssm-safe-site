What is SSM?

SSM stands for Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia, also known as the Companies Commission of Malaysia.

SSM is the statutory body responsible for the registration and administration of businesses and companies in Malaysia, and provides related corporate and business information through its official services.

Common SSM-related activities include registering a business, renewing a business registration, updating business information, obtaining business information, terminating a registered business, and handling certain compounds and compliance matters.

SSM is the authority, not the registration itself.

Malaysians commonly say “my SSM” when referring to their business registration, but SSM itself refers to Suruhanjaya Syarikat Malaysia.

Common Business Structures in Malaysia

Sole Proprietorship

A business owned by one individual.

Partnership

A business owned by two or more partners, subject to applicable registration requirements.

Company / Sdn. Bhd.

A company incorporated as a separate legal entity from its shareholders.

LLP / PLT

Limited Liability Partnership: a structure combining characteristics of a partnership with limited-liability features.

Different structures have different legal, tax, compliance and registration requirements. Refer to official SSM information or professional advice when choosing a structure.

What is SSM Renewal?

SSM business renewal extends the validity period of an eligible registered business, so it stays on record with SSM.

Before expiry

Eligible registrations may be renewed before they expire.

After expiry

Renewal may remain possible for up to 12 months after expiry, subject to current SSM rules.

Beyond the permitted period

Another process, such as registering again, may be required.

Renewal period: one to five years, depending on current requirements.

Who this applies to: sole proprietorships and partnerships. Companies and LLPs follow different requirements, so check with SSM.

Who Can Use EzBiz?

Owners must be Malaysian citizens or permanent residents aged 18 or above. All business owners and partners must register and verify their EzBiz user accounts. SSM's current guidance directs users through the SSM4U portal.

Official EzBiz Access & Eligibility

Download or Replace Documents

Business owners can download their certificate and business information, if purchased, from EzBiz within 90 days after approval. After that period, copies may be purchased through the service providers listed by SSM. Obtaining a copy does not renew the registration.

Official Download Guidance

Certificate or Business Profile?

A business registration certificate records the registration. A business profile provides registered particulars about the business. Choose the document requested by the organisation you are dealing with; a website lookup is not a replacement for an official document.

SSM Document Information

What Is Digital CTC?

A Digital Certified True Copy (DCTC) is a digitally certified document. SSM provides a DCTC reader and web checker to verify document authenticity. Our registration-status checker does not authenticate certificates.

Official DCTC Verification

Registration and Other Licences

SSM registration does not replace licences, permits or approvals required by a local authority or another relevant agency. Requirements depend on your business activity and location.

SSM Registration Guideline (PDF)

Late Renewal and Compounds

Late renewal can involve a compound in addition to renewal fees. Check any outstanding amount through official SSM services. Do not assume that registering a business again clears obligations connected with an earlier registration.
